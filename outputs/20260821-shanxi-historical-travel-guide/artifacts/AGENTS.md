# AGENTS.md

## Project purpose

Produce a Traditional Chinese travel guide for the Shanxi itinerary at:
https://www.travel.com.tw/TOU/TOU0020/ZWS1400048/ZCC070414BR26A

The guide must identify every visit spot in the published itinerary and explain its historical background, cultural significance, and on-site viewing priorities.

## Working rules

- Write reader-facing content in Traditional Chinese (Taiwan usage).
- Treat the travel itinerary page as the authority for route order and included attractions.
- Prefer primary and institutional sources: UNESCO, Chinese government cultural-heritage bodies, museum or site authorities, academic institutions, and official local gazetteers.
- Distinguish documented fact, scholarly interpretation, legend, and tourism storytelling.
- Cite factual historical claims with direct links; never cite search-result pages.
- Avoid long quotations. Paraphrase faithfully and record source titles and URLs.
- Preserve Chinese reign titles and dynastic dates where helpful, but translate specialist vocabulary into approachable prose.
- Explain not only “what happened,” but how architecture, religion, commerce, geography, and political power shaped each place.
- Do not invent operational facts such as current opening hours or ticket prices; verify any time-sensitive advice before publication.
- Treat superlatives such as “largest,” “oldest,” “only,” and “one of the world’s three” as claims requiring explicit verification.

## Deliverables

- `itinerary.md`: extracted day-by-day itinerary and normalized attraction checklist.
- `research.md`: sourced historical and cultural research notes.
- `guide.md`: final Traditional Chinese travel guide.
- `sources.md`: source register with titles, publishers, URLs, and access date.
- `handoff.md`: current state, decisions, remaining work, and verification notes.

## Quality bar

- Every named visit spot is represented, or explicitly marked as a transit, meal, accommodation, shopping, optional, or substitute item.
- Chronology, dynasties, names, and heritage designations are cross-checked.
- UNESCO properties are described with their correct inscription scope and criteria when relevant.
- The final guide separates practical itinerary context from historical interpretation.
- Legends and tourism narratives are labeled rather than presented as established events.
- All Markdown links resolve to the supporting page rather than a homepage or search result when possible.
